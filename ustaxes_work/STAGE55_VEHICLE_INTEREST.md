# Stage 55 — Schedule 1-A Part IV vehicle interest candidate

Adds an evidence-gated, non-filing-ready candidate calculator for 2025 qualified passenger vehicle loan interest, referencing the user-supplied IRS 2025 Form 1040 instructions, printed pages 109–110.

- Deductible interest is reduced by amounts already deducted elsewhere.
- Aggregate amount capped at $10,000; MAGI thresholds $100,000 (other statuses) / $200,000 (joint), with $200 reduction per $1,000 or fraction over threshold.
- Requires VIN, post-2024 loan origination, first lien, original use, personal use, vehicle eligibility, final US assembly, and loan review.
- Does not populate Form 1040 line 13b; does not authorize filing.
- VIN format check is not a complete VIN checksum/vehicle provenance verification. Evidence flags must be supported by actual documents. Refinancing, special inheritance exceptions, mixed-use allocation and vehicle-level evidence require further review.
- Full React build and Jest suite may require installing project dependencies; do not assume passed unless run.
