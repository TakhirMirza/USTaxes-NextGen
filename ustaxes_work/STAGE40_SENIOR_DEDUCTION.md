# Stage 40 — 2025 Schedule 1-A senior deduction candidate

Added `schedule1ASeniorDeduction2025.ts` and six regression scenarios.
The module calculates a **candidate** $6,000 per qualifying individual (up to $12,000 on joint returns), with a 6% phaseout above $75,000 MAGI ($150,000 joint). It rejects inconsistent filing and eligibility facts.

This does **not** certify Schedule 1-A eligibility or write Form 1040 line 13b. The remaining Schedule 1-A rules, exact worksheet rounding, identity checks, and full IRS tax table still need independent verification. Filing is blocked.
