# Schedules 2025 integrity review

Adds independent source-total vs Form 1040 candidate comparison for Schedule 1, 2, 3. Uses integer cents; discrepancies are diagnostics and do not post to a tax return. Does not assume source totals are legally reportable: worksheet calculations, tax rules, limits and cross-form dependencies remain outstanding. Includes two Jest tests. Existing routes, engine, and GitHub workflows are unchanged.
