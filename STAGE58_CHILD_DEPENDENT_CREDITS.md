# Stage 58 — Child and Other Dependent Credit review (2025)

Adds a conservative, explicitly non-filing-ready credit review with $2,200 per eligible qualifying child under 17 and $500 per eligible other dependent, and $50 per $1,000 (or fraction) MAGI phaseout above $200,000 ($400,000 married filing jointly). Requires documented identity and dependent checks; rejects duplicates. Does **not** calculate refundable Additional Child Tax Credit, Schedule 8812 line ordering, or post a credit to Form 1040 line 19. These remain blockers. Preliminary tax cap is illustrative and cannot replace the official Credit Limit Worksheet.

Seven Jest tests are included. This module is isolated until the complete Schedule 8812 and Form 1040 pipeline can be validated.
