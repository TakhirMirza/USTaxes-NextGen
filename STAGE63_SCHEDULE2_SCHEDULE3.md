# Stage 63 — Schedule 2 and Schedule 3 review (2025)

Adds an evidence-gated review model, separate from official return posting.

- Schedule 2 Part I (line 3) flows to Form 1040 line 17, **not** line 23.
- Schedule 2 Part II (line 21) flows to Form 1040 line 23.
- Schedule 3 Part I flows to Form 1040 line 20.
- Schedule 3 Part II flows to Form 1040 line 31; explicit duplicate-payment confirmation required.
- Null is not zero. Reviewed amounts require a source reference, including zero amounts.
- Does not implement individual Schedule 2/3 worksheets or eligibility, and does not authorize filing.
- Integration with line 18 tax after Schedule 2 Part I and downstream credit ordering remains future work.
