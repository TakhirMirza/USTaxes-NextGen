# NextGen 2.0 — Consolidated 2025 Readiness Review

Adds a unified, actionable review checklist across taxpayer profile, W-2/1099-INT source integrity, deduction eligibility and Form 1040 reconciliation. Includes diagnostic CSV export without names or financial amounts and links back to the appropriate workflow section.

This module **does not compute new tax amounts**, certify filing eligibility or enable e-file. It always blocks filing. Other tax years remain unsupported. Do not use actual taxpayer data in the development preview.
