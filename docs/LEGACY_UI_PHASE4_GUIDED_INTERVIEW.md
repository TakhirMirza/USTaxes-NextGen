# NextGen 2.0 — Guided Tax Interview (Phase 4)

- Restores a more task-focused interview experience to Overview, About You, Income, and Deductions.
- Adds a visible five-item completion checklist and navigation.
- Adds an explicitly fictional two-W-2 and one-1099-INT training example, loaded only after confirmation.
- Keeps eligibility confirmation flags unchecked and filing disabled.
- Preserves existing source calculations, engine and developer tools.
- The package intentionally does not introduce names, SSNs, addresses, or real taxpayer storage.
- No new tax formulas or IRS filing features.
