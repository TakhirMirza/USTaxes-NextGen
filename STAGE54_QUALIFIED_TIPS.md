# Stage 54 — Qualified tips candidate (2025)

Source: user-provided IRS 2025 Form 1040 instructions, Schedule 1-A Part II, printed pages 101–105. This module only accepts verified, already deduplicated employee tips and self-employed tips already reduced by the net-income limit **per business**. It does not determine whether an occupation is eligible or whether particular payments qualify; those must be verified upstream. For joint returns, the eligible income threshold is $300,000; otherwise $150,000. $25,000 annual cap and $100 reduction per $1,000 or fraction of excess MAGI. All filing outputs remain blocked and line 13b remains null.

The source project is not production-ready. Run a full dependency install, build and full test suite before release.
